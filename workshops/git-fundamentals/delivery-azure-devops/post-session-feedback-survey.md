## Facilitator Follow-Up

- Review responses within two business days.
- Contact anyone who requests help with Azure DevOps access or an unresolved lab issue.
- Record repeated questions in [faq.md](faq.md).
- Convert the highest-frequency topic into the opening example or a backup slide for the next session.
- Keep a short summary of scores and themes with the session planning notes; do not include personal contact details in shared materials.

## Recommended Form Settings

- Enable anonymous responses unless named follow-up is important.
- Mark Questions 1-3 as required; leave written responses optional.
- Do not collect more personal information than needed for follow-up.
- Add a completion message with the links to the [cheat sheet](cheat-sheet.md) and the next-session announcement.
